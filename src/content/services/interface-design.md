## The states nobody screenshots

A portfolio full of happy-path screens is a portfolio of screens your users will rarely
see. The interesting design problems live in the states that are hard to make look good:
the empty dashboard on day one, the list with four hundred rows, the upload that failed at
80%, the name that is far too long for its container.

Those get drawn here as a matter of course, because they are what your product actually
feels like to use.

## How it usually runs

Six weeks for a full product engagement. Week one is listening, week two is structure on
paper, weeks three to five are full-fidelity work, and week six is handover.

## What you get

Source files, a component library with every state documented, exported assets, and a
written specification covering behaviour and edge cases.
