## What this is for

Most companies do not have a brand problem. They have a coordination problem. The product
team, the marketing team and whoever made the last deck each solved the same visual
question differently, and nobody was wrong — they just never compared notes.

Art direction here means holding one answer across all of it, and writing that answer down
in a form the team can apply after I have gone.

## How it usually runs

Four to six weeks, depending on how many surfaces there are. The first week is spent
looking rather than making. Then a direction is proposed, argued about, and agreed — the
arguing is the useful part, because a direction nobody pushed back on is usually a
direction nobody will follow.

## What you get

A documented visual language, worked examples on your real surfaces, and a standing
review slot for as long as the engagement runs.
